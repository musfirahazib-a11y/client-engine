What I build

AI Agents

Task-specific agents that each own a single job end to end — sales and product recommendation, customer support and returns, abandoned-cart recovery, and lead qualification and booking. Each one runs an intent-driven conversation rather than a fixed script.

AI Sales Funnel System

A capture-to-conversion system: an AI assistant on the landing page, in-chat lead capture, configurable qualification with per-answer scoring, catalogue-grounded recommendations, live offers, booking or checkout, automated follow-up and abandoned-lead recovery — behind a CRM and analytics console.

Industry AI Systems

The agents and funnel configured and packaged for a specific industry (for example salon & spa, real estate or e-commerce), sharing one schema with per-vertical branding, listings, qualification, offers and follow-up.

AI-Powered Web

Conversion-focused, responsive, accessible front-ends, designed and built to a premium standard and structured so AI agents and automation plug into the customer journey.

The flagship: AI Sales Funnel System

One connected system where every stage is the same machine instead of a stack of disconnected tools. It is config-driven, so it retargets to a new industry by editing one object.

  • Visitor → AI engagement → qualification → recommendation
  • Sales conversation → purchase or booking
  • Automated follow-up → abandoned-lead and cart recovery
  • CRM, funnel analytics, follow-up and recovery in one console

Live, interactive demonstrations of all of the above are on musfirahloom.com.

How an engagement works

Typical sequence
StageWhat happens
1 · DiscoveryYou complete a short discovery questionnaire; we have one call to scope the work and agree what success looks like.
2 · ProposalYou receive a written proposal — scope, deliverables, approach, timeline and a fixed fee — plus a service agreement.
3 · BuildThe system is built in agreed stages, with a working preview you can review at each stage.
4 · ReviewYou test against the agreed scope; changes within scope are made before handover.
5 · HandoverYou get the working system, the source, and a short written guide for running and updating it.

What you get

  • A defined system that owns a specific workflow, not a vague "AI project"
  • Dependency-free front-end code (HTML, CSS, JavaScript) you can host anywhere
  • The conversation logic, configuration and admin views that sit around it
  • A written handover guide
What this is not. I do not sell guaranteed business outcomes. A system can be designed to improve engagement, qualification and follow-up, but I do not promise specific revenue, conversion, traffic or ranking figures, and I do not quote results I cannot evidence. Any numbers in a proposal are your targets, described as targets.

A good fit

Likely not a fit
