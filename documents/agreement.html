  1. Confidentiality

    Each party will keep the other’s non-public information confidential and use it only to perform this Agreement. This does not apply to information that is public, already known, independently developed, or required to be disclosed by law. This clause survives termination for years.

  2. Warranties & disclaimer

    • The Provider warrants that the services will be performed with reasonable skill and care, and that deliverables will materially conform to the SOW at acceptance. The Provider will correct non-conforming work reported within days of acceptance, at no charge.
    • No outcome guarantee. The Provider does not warrant or guarantee any specific business result, including revenue, sales, conversion rate, lead volume, traffic, search ranking, engagement or cost saving. Any figures in the SOW are the Client’s targets.
    • AI and other third-party model behaviour can vary and is not fully deterministic. Outputs should be reviewed by the Client before relying on them for decisions.
    • Except as stated above, the deliverables are provided “as is” after acceptance and all other warranties, express or implied, are excluded to the fullest extent permitted by law.

  3. Limitation of liability

    Neither party is liable for indirect, incidental, special or consequential loss, or for lost profits, revenue, data or goodwill. Each party’s total aggregate liability under this Agreement is limited to the total fees paid by the Client under the SOW in the months before the event giving rise to the claim. Nothing limits liability that cannot be limited by law.

  4. Indemnity

    The Client will indemnify the Provider against claims arising from content, materials, data or instructions the Client supplies, and from the Client’s use of the deliverables in breach of this Agreement or applicable law.
