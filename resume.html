Problem
Funnels leak — visitors arrive unqualified, follow-up is a generic drip, and abandoned leads and carts are never worked.
Built
One connected system: AI sales assistant, in-chat lead capture, configurable qualification with per-answer scoring, a transparent lead score, catalogue-grounded recommendations, live offers with real expiry, real booking or checkout, automated follow-up with stop conditions, and abandoned-lead / cart recovery — behind a CRM and analytics console.
Technology
JavaScript (ES Modules), config-driven architecture, intent-loop conversational engine, five-axis qualification engine, namespaced local persistence. No framework.
Purpose
Designed to help businesses capture, qualify, convert and retain customers with fewer manual hand-offs.

About

How I work.

I design and build AI-powered systems around the way a business actually operates — not AI features bolted onto a product, but connected systems that move a customer from first visit to purchase and back again.

I own the whole build: the agent conversation logic, the funnel, the qualification and scoring engine, the per-industry configuration, the CRM and analytics console, and the front-end around it. The codebase is dependency-free HTML, CSS and JavaScript (ES Modules) with a shared design system, deployable to any static host.

I work remotely with international clients and teams, and I'm comfortable taking a system from architecture through deployment.
