# Target Job Titles — Misbah Azib

## The 10 strongest titles to search for

Ranked by fit to your demonstrable work (AI agents + AI sales funnel + web) and by
volume of international remote postings.

1. **AI Automation Specialist** — your primary title; strongest keyword-to-evidence match.
2. **AI Automation Consultant** — same work, framed for agencies and client engagements.
3. **AI Agent Developer** — direct match to the four-agent suite.
4. **AI Solutions Specialist** — broad; covers agents + funnel + integration.
5. **AI Workflow Automation Specialist** — matches the funnel orchestration and follow-up engine.
6. **AI Sales Automation Specialist** — matches the sales funnel, recommendations, cart recovery.
7. **AI Implementation Specialist** — for roles delivering AI into an existing business.
8. **AI Integration Specialist** — for roles connecting AI to web / CRM / commerce systems.
9. **Automation Consultant** — wider net; your systems thinking still leads.
10. **AI-Powered Web Developer** — where the web build is the main ask and AI is the differentiator.

not "I use AI tools." Order the résumé so *AI Automation → AI Agents → AI Sales Systems →
Business Automation → Web Solutions* is the first thing a reader sees.
