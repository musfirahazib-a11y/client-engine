Our story

Started in a kitchen. Still small on purpose.

AÉRA began when its founder got tired of ten-step routines that promised transformation and delivered irritation. The brief was simple: the fewest effective products, made transparently, priced without theatre.

Everything is blended in batches of a few hundred units, dated, and sold until it runs out. When a formula can't be improved, it stays exactly as it is.
