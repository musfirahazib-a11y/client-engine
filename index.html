Questions

Good to know before we start.

What exactly do you build?

Conversion-focused business websites, e-commerce stores, and AI website assistants as an optional upgrade. Every engagement starts with a short call to scope the work.

Websites typically run $650–$2,400, e-commerce $1,400–$5,500, and an AI assistant add-on from $600. You get a fixed quote after the discovery call, not a surprise invoice.

How long does it take?

Most projects are 2–4 weeks from kickoff, depending on scope and how quickly content is ready.

Are the portfolio projects real clients?

They're concept builds — real front-ends for fictional brands, made to show the standard of the work. They're labelled as demos throughout; no client names or results are implied.

What happens after launch?

You own the site, the code and the accounts. An optional monthly care or growth plan keeps it healthy, updated and improving.
